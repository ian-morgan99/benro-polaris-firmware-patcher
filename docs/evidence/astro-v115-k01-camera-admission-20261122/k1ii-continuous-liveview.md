One long-lived v115 driver session (0f2f34e, research capture enabled), K-1 II 25fb:0183 in Manual. Enabled the session-local keep-live-view toggle, started PC live view once and requested frames continuously for 12 seconds, with a 33 ms pause between requests. No still capture. JPEG payloads were checked for start/end markers in memory. This measured delivery to the host, not Android presentation, scene-to-screen latency or the rate of unique sensor exposures.

276 frame payloads returned, 198 distinct byte lengths. First frame at 1.418 s. Excluding startup and the deliberately interposed shutter transaction, median frame delivery gap was 34.7 ms, p95 40.0 ms, maximum 48.9 ms. Frame 104 arrived 777.6 ms after frame 103. The 1 s restoration passed in session; a separate session confirmed shutter 00:01, conditions 1/1, idle state 0. Session teardown took 2.228 s.
