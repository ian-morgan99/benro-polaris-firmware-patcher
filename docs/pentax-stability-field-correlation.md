# Correlating lab failure recipes to field logs

Once an experiment produces a stable first-divergence fingerprint, search paired field Mlog/Clog for the same ordered signature.

Do not require field testers to reproduce engineering steps or diagnose layers. Their job remains: camera model, rough action/time, visible symptom, recovery and raw logs.

Our job is to map those reports to lab fingerprints. If a field report has only the same late symptom but lacks the initiating signature, keep attribution uncertain rather than forcing it into an existing defect.
